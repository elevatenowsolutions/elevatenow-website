Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

At ElevateNow ("we," "us," or "our"), we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the data you provide to us through our website, elevatenow.com.

By using our website, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you interact with our website. This includes:

A. Client & Inquiry Data

When you submit a form on our "Contact" page or request a discovery call, we collect:

  • Name (First and Last)
  • Work Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Company Name
  • Any message or details you provide regarding your project

B. Job Applicant Data

When you submit an application via our "Careers" page, we collect:

  • Name and Contact Information (Email, Phone)
  • Cover Note / Message
  • Resume/CV and any other documents you choose to upload (PDF, DOC, DOCX)

C. Automatically Collected Data

Like most websites, we may use cookies and similar technologies to collect basic technical data (such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited) to ensure our site functions properly and to analyze site traffic.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries: We use your contact details to schedule calls, answer your questions, and provide requested information about our ServiceNow consulting services.
  • To process job applications: We review resumes and cover notes to evaluate your qualifications for current or future employment opportunities.
  • To improve our website: We use anonymized analytics to understand how users navigate our site.
  • To comply with legal obligations: We may process data to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share your data only with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business (e.g., website hosting providers, email service providers, or applicant tracking systems), provided those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.

  • Client Inquiries: Retained for as long as necessary to manage our business relationship.
  • Job Applications: Resume data is retained for a period of 12 to 24 months to consider you for future roles, unless you request deletion.

5. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location (including under the GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (e.g., withdrawing a job application).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@elevatenow.com.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
